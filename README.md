# Dashboards

# 1. SaaS Inc. Power BI Dashboard  

## Overview  
This Power BI project analyzes SaaS Inc.'s sales performance across various products, regions, and customer segments. It provides key insights into revenue, profit margins, and product performance over multiple time periods.  

## Key Features  
- **Sales & Profit Analysis**: Tracks total revenue ($2.3M), total profit ($286.4K), and units sold (38K).  
- **Segment & Customer Insights**: Breaks down sales performance across SMB, Strategic, and Enterprise segments.  
- **Product Performance**: Compares sales and profit across SaaS products, highlighting top-performing items like ContactMatcher and FinanceHub.  
- **Regional Breakdown**: Analyzes total sales across NAMER, EMEA, APAC, and other global markets.  
- **Customer Profitability**: Identifies the most profitable customers and those contributing the most to sales.  

## Data Visualizations  
- Time-series analysis of sales by year, quarter, and month.  
- Profit % comparison across customers and products.  
- Total sales and profit distribution across industries.  
- Sales trends by region and division.
